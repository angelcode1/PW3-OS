# R3877.19.1 — eMMC boot-area parser correction

Research/educational purposes only. **Immutable hold: `NOT_BOOTABLE`.** No Kindle boot, firmware write, raw flash access, mount, backup, or recovery verification.

## What failed and why

The user's R3877.19 read-only SSH inventory completed successfully (SSH exit 0) and reported seven block devices: `mmcblk0`, `mmcblk0p1`–`mmcblk0p4`, `mmcblk0boot0`, `mmcblk0boot1`. The original Mac analyzer rejected both standard eMMC boot-area names as `INVALID_PARTITION_RECORD` because its regex accepted `mmcblkN` and `mmcblkNpN`, but not `mmcblkNboot[01]`. This is a local parser bug, **not evidence of a physical storage or filesystem failure**.

R3877.19.1 adds only `mmcblk\d+boot[01]` to the allowed names (retaining strict record shape and numeric fields) and informational block-device classification. It explicitly keeps recovery, bootloader location, physical root backing device, image backup, and bootability **unverified**. The original Kindle-side collector is byte-identical to the R3877.19 collector.
